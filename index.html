Play with w₂₃₁ — what the gradient actually means

The gradient is the slope of the dashed tangent. At w₂₃₁ = 2 the slope is +0.59: increasing w₂₃₁ raises the loss, because it pushes probability toward class 3, which is the wrong class. Gradient descent therefore moves w₂₃₁ down: w ← w − η·0.59.

Values are rounded to two or three decimals as in the exam solution. Exact: h₁ = 0.8808, ŷ₃ = 0.6729, ∂L/∂w₂₃₁ = 0.5927.
